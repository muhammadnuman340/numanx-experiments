How it works

  1. Resize. Your photo is shrunk to 160 × 160 pixels: 76,800 numbers.
  2. See. EfficientNet-B0, a network pretrained on over a million ImageNet photos, turns it into 1,280 numbers describing what it sees: fur, ears, eyes, face shape.
  3. Decide. A single trained neuron weighs those 1,280 numbers and outputs the probability of "dog".
  4. Answer. Above 50% means dog; below means cat.

About the model

Architecture
EfficientNet-B0 (frozen) + 1 sigmoid neuron
Trained on
18,610 photos

98.80% on 2,326 unseen photos

0.9986

Your device, with TensorFlow.js

Good to know

  • It only knows two answers. Show it a person or a car and it will still pick cat or dog.
  • Works best when one animal is clearly visible and fills much of the photo.
  • Hard cases: tiny animals far away, very dark cats, fluffy small dogs, and photos with both a cat and a dog.
