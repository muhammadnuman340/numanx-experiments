    Why this answer?

      Runs on your device. Trained on 10 animals and humans; other animals should be "Unknown animal"; drawings and objects should be "Not a real animal or human". Accuracy and limits: see the report.

      How it decides

      1. Resize. The whole photo is squashed to 224 × 224 pixels, just like in training.
      2. See. MobileNetV2 turns it into 1,280 numbers describing what it sees.
      3. Score. A trained layer scores 13 answers: 10 animals, human, unknown animal, and not a real animal or human.
      4. Check. Safety rules turn unsure or unfamiliar guesses into "Unknown", and a likely person is always answered "Human".

      About the model

      Architecture
      MobileNetV2 + 13-way softmax
      Knows
      10 animals and humans
      Test accuracy
      94.8% on 2,535 test photos
      Download size
      ≈ 10 MB, once
      Runs on
      Your device, with TensorFlow.js

      Good to know

      • One answer per photo. A person holding a cat gets a single answer.
      • Look-alikes are hard: a tiger or leopard may come out as "Cat", a moth as "Butterfly".
      • Statues, mannequins and realistic paintings of people may be called "Human".
